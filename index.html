I am a Security Consultant and Penetration Tester with 2.5+ years of experience delivering offensive security assessments across enterprise, financial, and infrastructure environments.

My journey into cybersecurity began from a non-traditional background. After completing a BCom with Computers, I developed a strong interest in technology and security, which led me to pursue a Diploma in Cyber Security and begin vulnerability research through bug bounty programs. This resulted in Hall of Fame recognition from organizations including Dell, BigBasket, Swiggy, Viator, TripAdvisor, and several others.

Since then, I have built hands-on experience performing Web, API, Mobile, Internal & External Network, Infrastructure, and ATM security assessments. My work has involved identifying and validating vulnerabilities such as SQL Injection, XSS, SSRF, IDOR, Authentication & Authorization flaws, Privilege Escalation, Rate-Limit Bypass, and Infrastructure Misconfigurations across enterprise-scale environments.

I have worked with leading stock exchange and banking clients, conducting Black Box, Gray Box, and White Box security assessments, vulnerability assessments, and Red Team exercises. In addition, I have participated in cyber drills and Purple Team activities, collaborating with defensive teams to improve detection, response, and overall security resilience.

My certifications include CEHv11, eJPT, and CRTP, with CRTO currently in progress. To further strengthen my expertise, I am currently pursuing an MSc in Cyber Security in Ireland, focusing on advanced attack simulation, offensive security techniques, and enterprise security resilience.

Through this portfolio, I share my research, projects, write-ups, certifications, and learning journey. My goal is simple: continuously improve as a security professional while helping organizations identify risks before attackers do.
